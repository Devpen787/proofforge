# The Proof Code

ProofForge is a cooperative workbench for useful software work.

We are here to build together, prove the work, share credit, and grow the commons.

## Our Code

1. No proof, no credit.
2. Builders do not grade their own work.
3. Maintainers receive evidence, not noise.
4. Small missions beat giant drops.
5. Human judgment guards public action.
6. Credit follows accepted usefulness.
7. Commercial work can fund the commons.
8. The work must hold.

## Working Norms

- Be direct and useful.
- Respect maintainers' time.
- Do not spam repositories with raw agent output.
- Credit contributors accurately.
- Keep disagreements tied to evidence and project goals.
- Prefer small, reviewable work over impressive-looking chaos.

When the evidence is good and the contribution is useful:

> The work holds.
